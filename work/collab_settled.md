# Settled collaboration items — archive

The closed half of `collab.md`. Items move here once their question has an answer everyone has seen,
so the open file stays short enough to read in one sitting.

**Union-merged like `collab.md`** — `CLAUDE.md`, "Formatting for union merge", applies here
unchanged: unique heading, unique closing stamp with a time, append rather than edit.

**Item numbers run as one sequence across `collab.md` and this file**, never restarting. Two people
taking the same number is the collision union merge cannot show you.

**Only the skills that sweep move an item here.** Moving one by hand skips the sweep's audits — the
duplicate-line check, the structure check, and the check that the item carries a disposition. An
item settled outside a sweep gets its answer appended, is marked settled, and **stays in
`collab.md`** until the next sweep collects it.

**Nothing here is edited after it lands.** A settled item that turns out wrong is a new item in
`collab.md`, not a correction here.
