# Settled collaboration items — archive

The closed half of `collab.md`. Items move here once their question has an answer everyone has seen,
so the open file stays short enough to read in one sitting.

**Union-merged, exactly like `collab.md`** — the formatting rules in `CLAUDE.md`
("Formatting for union merge") apply here unchanged: a unique heading, a unique closing stamp
carrying a time, append rather than edit.

**Item numbers are drawn from `collab.md` and this file together** — one sequence across both, never
restarting. Two people taking the same number is the collision that union merge cannot show you.

**Only the skills that sweep should move an item here.** Moving one by hand skips the audits that
run as part of the sweep — the duplicate-line check, the structure check, and the check that the
item actually carries a disposition. An item settled outside a sweep gets its answer appended, gets
marked settled, and **stays in `collab.md`** until the next sweep collects it.

**Nothing here is edited after it lands.** If a settled item turns out to be wrong, that is a new
item in `collab.md`, not a correction here.
